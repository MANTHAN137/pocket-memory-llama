Pocket Memory · Privacy

Llama runs on your phone. Saved memories, conversations and tasks stay in this app's storage. This edition includes no account or billing service and adds no analytics. Google ML Kit powers local photo text recognition and may send SDK diagnostic metrics when online; your photos and recognized text are not uploaded.

Model download
Pocket Memory downloads the pinned Llama file from Hugging Face through its Android foreground service. The file stays in app-managed storage. Its size and SHA-256 are checked before use. Pause or resume inside Models; no file import is needed.

Optional web search
When Search the web is enabled in Chat, only the message you send goes to Exa's MCP service at mcp.exa.ai. Saved memories and conversation history are not attached. Exa has its own policy: https://exa.ai/privacy-policy. Llama reads returned excerpts on your phone. Opening a source link uses your browser. The latest web answer and its references are saved locally.

Optional browser companion
Opening the companion starts a connection on this phone only, for up to 15 minutes. Browser agents you allow can read matching saved memories and ask local Llama. Your chosen browser agent may process returned content under its own provider's policy. The companion cannot edit or delete memories, purchase anything or download models. Turn it off from its notification or Settings. No session token is stored in cookies or local storage.

Storage
Android cloud backup and device transfer are excluded. The database has no additional app-level encryption; Android's app and device protection apply. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes local data and downloaded models. Exported backups are files you control. Answers may be wrong; inspect the evidence before relying on them.
